Our story

Every woman deserves happiness, friendship and the chance to rediscover herself.

Her Next Life was created with a simple belief: every woman deserves happiness, friendship, personal growth and opportunities to rediscover herself at every stage of life.

We want to create a welcoming space where women aged 40+ can come together, build connections, explore interests and celebrate life. Whether you are looking to try something new or simply share a conversation, there is room for you here.
